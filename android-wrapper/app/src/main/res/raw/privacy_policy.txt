Xianzhun privacy policy — native Android app
Updated / effective: 15 October 2026 · Policy version 1

1. Scope and contact
This policy describes the native Android app io.github.lj5201lj.xianzhun, version 2.3.0. The public PWA and external websites are separate products. Project maintenance account: GitHub lj5201lj. Questions and privacy requests can be raised at:
https://github.com/lj5201lj/guitar-tuner-pwa/issues
GitHub issues are public and may require an account. Do not include recordings, identity information, passwords or private contact details. The operator has not supplied a legal publishing name or support email. These remain release prerequisites for a formal app-store submission; this test release does not assert store compliance.

2. Microphone permission and audio
Only tuning and the microphone self-test request RECORD_AUDIO. The app explains the purpose before the Android permission request. You can decline; reference tones and the metronome remain available. Recording starts only through your action and stops when you stop it, switch tools, change language, or leave the foreground.
PCM audio is temporarily processed in memory to estimate pitch, volume and distortion. The self-test keeps only temporary numerical counters. Microphone audio is not saved as a file, retained as history, sent to a server, used to train models, or shared with third parties. Memory buffers are discarded after the recording session. Reference playback temporarily suspends analysis while the microphone connection may remain open; Android may continue showing its microphone indicator until you stop tuning.

3. Local settings
Tuning choice, one custom tuning, selected string, automatic/manual mode, language, metronome settings, haptic preference and the microphone-use notice status are stored locally. No login, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or app-operated server is used. This version disables Android app backup; platform-managed app language and manufacturer device migration are controlled by the OS, not the app.

4. Internet, feedback and clipboard
This APK has no INTERNET permission. Core features and in-app policy/licence pages work offline. If you explicitly open an online policy, licence or feedback link, Android opens an external browser. That website and browser may process connection/account information under their own policies; nothing is posted by the app automatically.
Copy diagnostics is optional. It copies only app version, Android version/API and a blank issue template to your system clipboard; it does not include audio, device IDs or logs. You choose whether to paste or share it. Clipboard access and browser history are governed by the OS/browser.

5. Your choices and deletion
You can stop tuning at any time, revoke microphone permission in Android app settings, disable in-tune haptics, and continue using non-recording tools. Clear the app storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove app-owned preferences. Public feedback you voluntarily submit is managed separately on GitHub; use its controls or contact the project there to request assistance. Do not submit private information in a public issue.

6. Young users and safety
No age information or user profiles are collected. A young user should ask a guardian for help with permissions, public feedback and physical string adjustment. Verify the correct string and octave before tightening; automated detection is not a guarantee against overtightening or a broken string.

7. Updates
Material changes in audio processing, permissions or data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where required, a new explanation before use. App updates are obtained manually from the distribution channel; the app does not download updates itself.
